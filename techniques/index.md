# Techniques

We combine behavioural experiments, measures of brain function and non-invasive neuromodulation to investigate psychiatric disorders, with a particular focus on schizophrenia.

## Non-invasive Neuromodulation

Our work includes transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS), transcranial alternating current stimulation (tACS), their high-definition variants, and temporal interference stimulation. We use neuromodulation for research and clinical treatment.

## Electroencephalography (EEG)

We record the brain’s electrical activity to investigate neural processes during rest and cognitive tasks.

## Eye Tracking

We measure gaze patterns, eye movements and pupil responses to investigate visual attention and cognitive processing.

## Functional Near-infrared Spectroscopy (fNIRS)

We measure changes in oxygenated and deoxygenated haemoglobin to study brain haemodynamic responses.

## Psychophysics and Cognitive Experiments

We use controlled experimental tasks to examine perception, attention, memory, decision-making and other cognitive processes.

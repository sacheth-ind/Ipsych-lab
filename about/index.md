# About Our Lab

Our lab aims to advance understanding of the neurobiology of severe mental disorders, with a particular focus on schizophrenia. We combine cognitive, behavioural and electrophysiological approaches with neuromodulation to investigate underlying mechanisms and provide clinical treatment.
## Faculty

### Dr Ganesan Venkatasubramanian

**Faculty in Charge**  
MBBS, MD (Psychiatry), PhD  
Professor, Department of Psychiatry  
NIMHANS, Bengaluru

Dr Ganesan Venkatasubramanian’s research focuses on the neurobiology of schizophrenia, integrating neuroimaging, clinical psychopharmacology and the study of immune-metabolic mechanisms. His interests also include systems biology and evolutionary approaches to understanding psychiatric disorders.

### Dr Sreeraj V S

**Associate Professor, Department of Psychiatry**  
NIMHANS, Bengaluru

Dr Sreeraj V S is a psychiatrist and physician-scientist whose work focuses on neurocognitive and neurobiological markers of psychiatric disorders, translational psychiatry and brain stimulation. His training includes a postdoctoral fellowship in clinical neurosciences and therapeutics in schizophrenia at NIMHANS.
